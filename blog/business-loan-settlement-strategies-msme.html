Business Loan Settlement for MSMEs: Legal Strategies

March 30, 2024 | MSME Support Team

Running a business in India is challenging, and financial downturns are sometimes unavoidable. If your business loan has become a burden, understanding the legal path to settlement is crucial for the survival of your enterprise.

Secured vs. Unsecured Business Loans

The settlement approach varies depending on the loan type. Unsecured business loans are settled similarly to personal loans. However, for secured loans, banks may initiate SARFAESI Act proceedings. Professional legal intervention is often needed to halt such proceedings and pave the way for a negotiated settlement.

Key Strategies for MSMEs

  • Transparency in Financials: Show the bank genuine reasons for business failure (market changes, pandemic impact, client defaults).
  • One-Time Settlement (OTS): Most banks have annual OTS schemes for MSMEs. Our team monitors these schemes to get you the best deal.
  • Legal Shield for Proprietors: Protect your personal assets from recovery harassment while the business debt is being negotiated.

How We Help MSMEs

Our legal team acts as an intermediary, presenting a professional restructuring or settlement proposal to the bank. We ensure that you get a closure that allows you to restart or exit your business with dignity or minimal financial scarring.
