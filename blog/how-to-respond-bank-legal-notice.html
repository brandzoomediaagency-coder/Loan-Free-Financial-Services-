How to Respond to a Bank's Legal Notice

May 5, 2024 | Legal Drafting Team

Receiving a legal notice is often the first formal step a bank takes before starting litigation. While it looks scary, it is actually a vital opportunity for you to state your side of the story and propose a resolution.

Dos and Don'ts

  • DON'T Ignore It: Ignoring a notice can be seen as an admission of debt in court. Always reply within the stipulated time.
  • DO Verify the Facts: Check if the amounts mentioned (principal, interest, penalties) match your records.
  • DO Explain Your Hardship: Use the reply to formally document your financial crisis (e.g., medical bills, job loss).

The Power of a Legal Reply

A well-drafted reply by an advocate can often "stop the clock" on litigation. It signals to the bank that you are legally aware and ready to settle, but only on fair terms. Most banks prefer a negotiated settlement over expensive and time-consuming court battles once they receive a professional reply.
