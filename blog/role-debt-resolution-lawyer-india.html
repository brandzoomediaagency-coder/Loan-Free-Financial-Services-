The Role of a Debt Resolution Lawyer in India

April 5, 2024 | Legal Services Team

Debt resolution is not just a financial transaction; it's a legal process. Navigating the world of bank notices, recovery agents, and legal procedures requires specialized expertise that only a debt resolution lawyer can provide.

Why Hire a Lawyer?

While you can try to settle on your own, having a lawyer gives you several critical advantages:

  • Halting Harassment: Legally, once you have appointed a lawyer, recovery agents are bound to communicate only through your legal counsel. This stops the intrusive calls and visits.
  • Expert Notice Drafting: A lawyer knows exactly how to respond to a bank's legal notice to protect your interests and prevent litigation from escalating.
  • Negotiation Leverage: Banks take legal representation seriously. A lawyer can negotiate for deeper waivers and better settlement terms that an individual might not be able to secure.

Protecting Your Rights

A debt resolution lawyer ensures that the settlement letter issued by the bank is legally sound and that your account is properly closed in the records. They also help in dealing with specialized issues like Section 138 (cheque bounce) or SARFAESI notices.
