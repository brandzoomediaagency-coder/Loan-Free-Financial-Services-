Debt Relief for Seniors & Pensioners

May 25, 2024 | Seniors Advocacy Team

Retirement should be a time of peace, but many seniors in India find themselves trapped in debt due to medical expenses, family commitments, or failed investments. Dealing with banks and recovery agents in old age is particularly stressful.

Why Seniors Need Special Handling

Banks often treat seniors with more sensitivity, but recovery agents might not. Pension income is often the only source of survival, and protecting it is our top priority. Law protects basic pension from being completely attached for debt recovery in most cases.

Special Relief Options

  • Compassionate Settlement: We negotiate with banks based on age and health factors, often securing deeper waivers than for younger borrowers.
  • EMI Restructuring: Aligning repayment with the reduced post-retirement income.
  • Legal Protection from Harassment: Ensuring that senior citizens are not subjected to verbal abuse or frequent visits that could affect their health.

Advice for Families

If your parents are in debt, don't let them suffer in silence. Professional intervention can resolve the issue within months, giving them the peaceful retirement they deserve. We handle all communications, so they don't have to deal with recovery stress.
