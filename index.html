Debt Resolution FAQ

Common questions about settling your loans in India

Yes, debt settlement (One-Time Settlement or OTS) is a recognized legal process. Banks often prefer recovering a portion of the loan rather than nothing. We ensure your settlement is documented with a 'No Dues Certificate' to protect you legally.

Initially, a settlement is marked as 'Settled' on your CIBIL report, which is better than 'Default' but not as good as 'Closed'. However, once your debts are cleared, we provide a step-by-step roadmap to rebuild your credit score to 750+ within 12-18 months.

As your legal representatives, we issue a formal notice to the bank. Under RBI recovery guidelines, once a lawyer is appointed, agents must communicate with the legal counsel, not the borrower. We redirect all their calls to our specialized team.
