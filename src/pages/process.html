Our Process

A transparent, legal, and effective journey to financial freedom.

01

Consultation & Analysis

We analyze your loan agreements, income, and hardship status to determine your eligibility. Our experts explain your rights under the latest RBI guidelines.

Duration: 24 Hours
02

Documentation & Profiling

We collect necessary documents like KYC and Loan Statements to create a professional financial profile. This profile is key to presenting a strong case to your lenders.

Legal Representation Agreement
03

Legal Protection Activation

With your authorisation, our team helps coordinate creditor communication and supports you in raising unfair or unlawful recovery practices with the lender and the RBI under the Fair Practices Code.

Benefit: Respond to harassment lawfully
04

Negotiation Strategy

We provide negotiation support with your lenders to seek a workable settlement or restructuring. Any reduction depends on the lender's policy, your outstanding amount and hardship profile.

Goal: A workable settlement
05

Settlement Agreement

Once an offer is secured, the bank issues a formal Settlement Letter. Our legal experts scrutinize every clause to ensure a clean closing of your account.

Audit: No Hidden Clauses
06

Closure & Freedom

You make the final payment directly to the lender. We facilitate the receipt of your No Dues Certificate (NOC) and assist in updating your CIBIL status.

Outcome: NOC & CIBIL Update

Before You Start

Gather Documents

Keep your loan account statements and latest notices handy.

Don't Panic

Stop talking to rude agents. Tell them your lawyer will speak.

Start Saving
