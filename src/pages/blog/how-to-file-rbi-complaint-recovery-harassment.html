How to File an RBI Complaint Against Recovery Agent Harassment in India

Quick answer — how to complain about a recovery agent

Step 1: File a written complaint with your lender's Grievance Redressal Officer. Step 2: If unresolved within 30 days, escalate to the RBI Ombudsman at cms.rbi.org.in or call 14448. Step 3: For criminal conduct (threats, abuse, intimidation), file a police FIR. For digital lending app harassment additionally, report on cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930. There is no fee to use the RBI CMS portal.

Recovery agent misconduct — abusive calls, late-night calls, threatening messages, contacting your family — is not just wrong; it is a violation of the RBI Fair Practices Code and may additionally constitute a criminal offence. This guide explains exactly how to document violations, escalate complaints and access your legal remedies.

Before you complain — document everything

Strong documentation makes your complaint significantly more effective. Before filing, gather:

  • Call logs: Screenshot showing time, date and caller number. Save the log.
  • Call recordings: If you recorded the conversation (legal in India for personal use), save the file.
  • Messages/WhatsApp: Screenshot all threatening or abusive messages. Do not delete them.
  • Emails and letters: Save all written communications from the lender and recovery agency.
  • Witness details: Name and contact of any person who was present during a home visit or who received harassing calls.
  • Loan account details: Your account number, lender name, loan type and outstanding amount.

Step-by-step complaint escalation process

Step 01

Lender's Grievance Redressal Officer (GRO)

Where: The lender's official website must publish GRO contact details under their "Grievance Redressal" or "Customer Service" section.

How: File a written complaint (email preferred) stating the specific violations — dates, times, nature of harassment, agent details if known.

Timeline: The lender must acknowledge within 7 days and resolve within 30 days. You receive a complaint reference number.

Step 02

Lender's Nodal Officer (if GRO is unresponsive)

Where: Also listed on the lender's website.

When: If the GRO does not respond within 7 days or does not resolve within 30 days.

How: Forward your GRO complaint to the Nodal Officer with a note that the lower-level complaint was not resolved in time. Keep the GRO complaint reference.

Step 03

RBI Integrated Ombudsman — cms.rbi.org.in

Eligible complaints: Against banks and qualifying NBFCs (assets ≥ ₹100 crore) regulated by the RBI.

When to use: Only after exhausting the lender's internal process (or if the lender does not respond within 30 days).

How: File at cms.rbi.org.in. Alternatively, call 14448 for telephone assistance or write to your regional RBI office. Filing is free. You can track your complaint online with the reference number.

Required: Your complaint should include the GRO complaint reference number, the lender's response (or absence of it), and all evidence.

Step 04

Police FIR — for criminal conduct

When: If the recovery agent made criminal threats, used abusive/obscene language, intimidated physically, made false police impersonation, or engaged in extortion.

How: Visit your nearest police station or cyber police station. Bring your call log screenshots, recordings and any other evidence. Ask the police to register a First Information Report (FIR) under the applicable provisions of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (which replaced the IPC on 1 July 2024). Keep a copy of the FIR for your records.

For digital lending app harassment: Additionally file on cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930 (National Cyber Fraud Helpline).

Template — complaint to Grievance Redressal Officer

To: The Grievance Redressal Officer
[Bank/NBFC Name]
[GRO Email Address from lender's website]

Subject: Formal Complaint — Illegal Recovery Agent Conduct — Account No. [YOUR ACCOUNT NUMBER]

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to formally complain about the conduct of a recovery agent acting on behalf of [Bank/NBFC Name] regarding my loan account [Account Number].

Nature of violation: [Describe specifically — e.g., "The agent called me at [time] on [date], which is outside the RBI-permitted window of 8 AM to 7 PM. The agent used abusive language and threatened [describe]."

Evidence: I attach screenshots of my call log showing [describe]. [Attach call recordings, message screenshots as applicable.]

I request immediate action against the assigned recovery agent and a written confirmation that the violations will not recur. Please resolve this complaint within 30 days. If this complaint is not satisfactorily resolved within 30 days, I will escalate to the RBI Ombudsman at cms.rbi.org.in.

Yours faithfully,
[Your Name]
[Your Mobile Number]
[Date]
