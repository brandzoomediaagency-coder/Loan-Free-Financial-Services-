NBFC Loan Settlement in India

Support for borrowers with loans from non-banking finance companies (NBFCs), including loans taken through an NBFC's partner app.

Short answer

Yes, NBFC loans can be settled. NBFCs are regulated by the RBI and, like banks, must have a board-approved policy for compromise settlements. The NBFC decides whether to accept, usually after the account is overdue and the hardship is documented. A settled loan is reported as "Settled" on your credit report.

What makes NBFC loans different

A non-banking financial company lends money but does not hold a banking licence. Many personal loans, consumer durable loans and app-based loans in India are actually given by NBFCs, sometimes through a lending app or partner platform. The app is not always the lender — check your loan agreement or key fact statement to see which regulated entity actually lent you the money. That is the company you settle with and complain to.

The RBI's compromise-settlement framework (8 June 2023) applies to NBFCs as well as banks, and separate recovery directions for NBFCs (RBI/2026-27/230) take effect on 1 January 2027.

Who this helps

  • Borrowers with personal or consumer loans from an NBFC who can no longer pay the EMIs.
  • People with several small app-linked loans who need to know which lender is behind each one.
  • Small business owners with NBFC business loans facing a cash-flow gap.
  • Anyone facing aggressive recovery from an NBFC or its agents.

Eligibility and limitations

  • Settlement is the NBFC's decision. It usually considers it only when the account is overdue and the hardship is genuine.
  • We help with unsecured NBFC loans only. Secured loans, such as vehicle or gold loans, are not covered: they follow different rules because the lender can enforce its security.
  • The NBFC that settles with you must wait at least 12 months (for non-farm loans) before lending to you again.
  • If the "lender" is an app that is not linked to any RBI-regulated entity, it is not a legitimate lender — see loan app harassment support.

Documents to keep ready

  • Loan agreement, sanction letter or key fact statement
  • Repayment schedule and latest statement of account
  • PAN, address proof and proof of hardship
  • Screenshots of app messages, SMS and call logs from collection agents

Risks to understand

  • Penal charges and interest continue until you reach an agreement.
  • Some NBFC loans have arbitration clauses, so disputes may go to an arbitrator rather than a court. Read our arbitration guide.
  • Never pay a collection agent personally in cash, or into a personal UPI ID. Pay the NBFC through its official channels and get a receipt.
