Credit Score & Post-Settlement Guidance

Guidance on closure documentation and practical, realistic steps to rebuild your credit profile over time.

Quick answer

After a settlement, your account is usually reported as “Settled”, which can lower your credit score and remain on your report for several years. There is no instant fix and no guaranteed score. With correct closure documentation (such as an NOC) and disciplined credit behaviour over time, many borrowers gradually improve their profile. We guide you on the practical steps.

Closing a debt is the first step; rebuilding your credit health is the next. We help you make sure your closure is documented correctly and explain realistic habits that support a stronger profile over time.

Who this service is for

  • Borrowers who have settled or are about to settle a loan or card.
  • People who see a “Settled” status and want to understand and improve it.
  • Those who want correct closure documentation (NOC) and report accuracy.
  • Anyone seeking a realistic, no-hype roadmap to rebuild credit.

Documents usually required

  • Settlement letter / No Objection Certificate (NOC).
  • Latest credit report (for example CIBIL).
  • Details of current and past credit accounts.
  • Proof of any payments made toward closure.

Risks & limitations to understand

  • A “Settled” status can remain on your report for several years and cannot simply be erased.
  • No one can guarantee a specific score or an overnight increase.
  • Beware of anyone promising “guaranteed CIBIL repair” — that is a red flag.
  • Rebuilding takes consistent, disciplined behaviour over time.
Important: We do not guarantee any credit-score outcome and do not offer “instant” credit repair. We provide guidance on documentation, accuracy and realistic rebuilding habits.
