Editorial Policy

How we research, write, check and correct the information on this website.

Last updated: 1 November 2026. People often read our pages when they are worried about money. We hold ourselves to these rules so that what we publish is accurate, current and honest about its limits.

What our content is for

Our guides explain loan settlement, restructuring, recovery rules, legal notices and credit reports in plain language for borrowers in India. They are general information, not legal or financial advice for your situation. We are a debt-resolution consultancy — not a lender, a law firm or a credit bureau — and we are not approved by the Reserve Bank of India.

Who prepares and reviews it

Pages are prepared by the Loan Free Financial Services editorial team. Each page shows when it was last updated and whether it has had independent expert review. We are arranging review of our legal and regulatory pages by a qualified professional; until a page has been reviewed, it says "Independent expert review of this page is pending". When a reviewer is appointed, their name and qualification will appear on the pages they have reviewed.

Drafts may be prepared with the help of AI writing tools. Before publication, every statement about a law, rule or deadline must be checked against the official source, and we do not publish invented statistics, case results, quotes or credentials.

Our sources

For law and regulation we rely on primary sources, and we link to them in a "Sources" section on the page:

  • Reserve Bank of India directions, circulars and press releases — rbi.org.in — and the RBI complaint portal, cms.rbi.org.in.
  • Acts of Parliament as published on indiacode.nic.in, such as the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, the SARFAESI Act, 2002, the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
  • Official portals such as the National Legal Services Authority and the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
  • Credit bureaus' own published guidance for how disputes work.

We do not rely on news reports, social media or other consultancies' websites for statements of law.

What we will not publish

  • Promises or predictions of a waiver percentage, timeline or credit score.
  • Statements that we are approved, licensed or endorsed by the RBI or any authority.
  • Invented client stories, reviews, ratings, statistics or credentials.
  • Advice to stop paying a loan deliberately, or to mislead a lender.
  • Location or keyword pages created only for search engines.

Keeping pages up to date

When the RBI or Parliament changes a rule we write about — for example the recovery directions of 6 August 2026, which take effect on 1 January 2027 — we update the affected pages and their "Last updated" date. We change the date only when we make a substantive change, not for cosmetic edits.

Corrections

If you think something on this site is wrong or out of date, email info@athenalegalsolutionllp.in with the page address and the problem. We will check it against the official source and correct the page if needed.

Advertising and independence

We do not accept payment from lenders, apps or anyone else to publish content or links, and we do not carry third-party advertising. Links to external sites are chosen because they are official or useful sources.

Client feedback

We publish client feedback only with the client's permission, with no payment or incentive, and we do not edit it to change its meaning. Feedback describes a person's experience of our service, not a promised result.
