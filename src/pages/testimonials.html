How We Handle Client Feedback

We publish client stories only when they are verified and shared with written permission.

Debt problems are personal, and every case is different. A story about one client's settlement can easily be read as a promise about yours — so we are careful about what we publish.

Our approach

  • We publish feedback only with the client's written consent.
  • Names and details are anonymised unless the client asks otherwise.
  • Any case study describing an outcome is backed by documents and states that results vary.
  • We never pay or offer incentives for reviews, and we do not filter out negative ones.

Independent reviews will be linked here once our Google Business Profile is verified.
