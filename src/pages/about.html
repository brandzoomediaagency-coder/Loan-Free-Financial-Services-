About Loan Free Financial Services

A Delhi-based debt-resolution consultancy helping borrowers across India understand their options and deal with their lenders in writing. We are not a lender.

Who we are, in brief

Loan Free Financial Services is a debt-resolution consultancy with an office at Tis Hazari Court, Delhi. We help people who are struggling with personal loans, credit cards, NBFC or app loans and business loans to understand their options, organise their documents, ask their lenders for a settlement or restructuring, and respond lawfully to unfair recovery practices. Only a lender can agree to a settlement; we never promise one.

Key facts

Why we exist

Most people who fall behind on loans did not plan to. A job loss, a medical emergency or a business downturn can turn manageable EMIs into a crisis within months. What follows is often confusing: penalty charges, recovery calls, notices written in legal language, and offers that are hard to judge.

We believe debt is a financial problem, not a character flaw. Our job is to explain the realistic options in plain language, help you put your case to your lender properly, and make sure you understand every document before you sign or pay.

What we do — and what we do not do

We do

  • Explain your options and the rules lenders and agents must follow
  • Help you prepare a documented settlement or restructuring request
  • Help you respond to, record and escalate unfair recovery practices
  • Check settlement letters and closure documents with you
  • Coordinate with independent counsel where a matter needs an advocate

We do not

  • Lend money or arrange new credit
  • Promise a waiver percentage, timeline or credit-score result
  • Advise anyone to stop paying dues they can afford
  • Ask for OTPs, passwords, PINs or card numbers
  • Claim RBI approval — the RBI does not license settlement consultancies

How we work

  1. Listen first. We start with what caused the difficulty — job loss, illness, business losses — because it shapes what a lender may accept.
  2. Honest options. Settlement, restructuring, repayment or a legal response: we explain the costs, the credit-report effect and the risks of each.
  3. Everything in writing. Requests to lenders, offers and closure documents are kept in writing so you have a clear record.
  4. You decide. We inform and support. You choose whether and how to proceed, and payments go directly to your lender.
