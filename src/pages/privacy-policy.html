Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 1 November 2026. This policy explains how Loan Free Financial Services ("we", "us") handles personal data when you visit www.loanfree.info, send us an enquiry, or contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email.

1. Who we are

Loan Free Financial Services is a debt-resolution consultancy operated by Athena Legal Solution LLP, at Chamber No. 698, 2nd Floor, Western Wing, Tis Hazari Court, District Courts North, Delhi 110054. For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act"), we are the Data Fiduciary for the personal data you give us. We are not a lender, a bank or a credit bureau.

2. What we collect

When you send an enquiry through a form on this website

  • Your name, mobile number and city.
  • The topic you choose (for example "Personal loan" or "Recovery agent harassment"), an approximate amount range if you choose one, and how you prefer to be contacted.
  • An optional short message. We ask you not to include account numbers, card numbers or passwords, and long numbers in messages are removed automatically.
  • Your consent, the page and language of the form, and the time of the enquiry, so we can show what you agreed to and when.

Forms are sent over HTTPS to our server and then to our private enquiry system. They are not sent to any public spreadsheet, and their contents are never sent to analytics or advertising services.

When you call, WhatsApp or email us

We receive what you choose to tell us, along with your phone number or email address. WhatsApp messages are handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.

When you browse the website

See Cookies and analytics below. Our hosting provider also keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and secure the website. Our server briefly uses your IP address to limit repeated form submissions; we do not store it with your enquiry.

We never ask for internet-banking passwords, UPI PINs, OTPs, full card numbers or CVVs. Please do not send them to us.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you a free first consultation.
  • To provide the services you ask for, including — only on your instruction — corresponding with your lenders.
  • To keep records we need for our work and to meet legal obligations.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how the website is used (only with your consent; see below).

We process your data on the basis of the consent you give, which you can withdraw at any time, and where processing is needed for a service you have asked for or to meet a legal obligation.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent personal data. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us run the website and handle enquiries — our website host (Vercel) and our enquiry-management provider — under confidentiality obligations and only for that purpose.
  • With your lenders or their representatives, and with an independent advocate, only when you ask us to act or coordinate on your behalf.
  • Where the law requires it, for example in response to a valid order of a court or authority.

Some providers may store data on servers outside India. Where they do, we rely on the transfer rules under the DPDP Act.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as we need them to respond and, if you become a client, to provide our services and meet record-keeping obligations. If you do not go ahead, you can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

7. How we protect it

The website uses HTTPS. Enquiries are checked and cleaned on our server, protected against spam, and passed to our enquiry system with a secret signature so they cannot be forged in transit. Access is limited to the people who need it to help you. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the DPDP Act, 2023.

8. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct or complete it;
  • erase it when it is no longer needed;
  • stop contacting you, and withdraw your consent;
  • let someone you nominate exercise your rights if you die or become unable to.

Email info@athenalegalsolutionllp.in with your request. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children and other websites

Our services are for adults aged 18 and over. Links to other websites, such as RBI or government portals, are provided for information; those sites have their own privacy practices.
