What we offer, in one paragraph

We help borrowers who cannot keep up with their loans to understand their options and deal with their lenders properly: preparing a settlement or restructuring request, checking settlement letters and closure documents, and responding to unfair recovery practices and legal notices. We are a consultancy, not a lender. Only your lender can agree a settlement, and we never promise a waiver.

Who we usually help

  • Salaried people with several personal loans or cards whose income has fallen.
  • Small business owners and traders facing a cash-flow crunch.
  • Families dealing with medical costs or a job loss.
  • Anyone receiving recovery calls, visits or notices they do not understand.
  • Retired people and pensioners with loans taken while working — see debt options for seniors.
  • Families repaying education loans — see education loan repayment options.

When settlement may not be right

Settlement is not always the best option. If you can afford to repay with a longer tenure or a lower EMI, restructuring usually does less damage to your credit report. Secured loans such as home or vehicle loans follow different rules because the lender can enforce its security. We will tell you honestly if another route suits you better.

About fees

Your first consultation is free. Before you engage us, ask for our fees in writing and make sure you understand what they cover. Settlement amounts are always paid directly to your lender, never to us. Be cautious of any firm that asks for large fees upfront or promises a fixed waiver.
