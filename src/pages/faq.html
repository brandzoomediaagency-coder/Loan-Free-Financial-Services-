Find answers to common questions about debt settlement and your rights.

What is Debt Settlement?

Debt settlement is a process where we negotiate with your lenders to accept a one-time lump-sum payment that is less than the full amount you owe. This is typically done for unsecured debts like personal loans and credit cards.

Is Debt Settlement legal in India?

Yes, debt settlement is a purely commercial negotiation between a borrower and a lender. While there is no specific "Debt Settlement Act," it is a widely recognized practice by banks and NBFCs to recover dues from distressed borrowers under RBI's compromise settlement guidelines.

Will it affect my CIBIL score?

Initially, yes. A settlement is reported as "Settled" on your credit report, which is better than "Written Off" but not as good as "Closed." However, for someone already defaulting or struggling with EMIs, settlement provides a way to stop further damage and start rebuilding credit over time.

Can you stop recovery agent harassment?

Absolutely. One of our primary roles is to act as your legal shield. We ensure that all communications from lenders follow RBI's Fair Practices Code, which strictly prohibits harassment, abusive language, or visits at odd hours.

What types of loans can be settled?

We primarily assist with unsecured debts, including:

  • Personal Loans
  • Credit Card Dues
  • Business Loans (Unsecured)
  • NBFC App Loans (Fintech Loans)
