We meet clients from Delhi at our office and work with people across the National Capital Region — including Noida, Greater Noida, Gurugram, Ghaziabad and Faridabad — by phone, WhatsApp and email. You do not need to visit in person, and clients outside the NCR are supported the same way.

How we work

  1. Free consultationIn person or by phone. We listen and explain your options honestly, including when settlement is not the right choice.
  2. Documents and planWe help you collect what the lender needs and agree an approach and an amount you can arrange.
  3. Lender communicationWe help you put your request in writing and raise any unfair recovery practices.
  4. Checking and closureWe check settlement letters before you pay, and guide you on the NOC and credit report afterwards.

Our office is at a court complex — what that means

Our chamber is inside the Tis Hazari Court complex. We are a debt-resolution consultancy, not a law firm, and our address there does not mean we act for any court.
