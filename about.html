About Loan
Free Financial Services

Legal-backed debt resolution and loan harassment protection for borrowers across India. Based in Delhi, serving families nationwide.

In the rapidly evolving financial landscape of India, thousands of honest borrowers find themselves trapped in an overwhelming cycle of debt. What starts as a manageable EMI often spirals into a crisis due to sudden job losses, medical emergencies, or business downturns. Beyond the financial weight, the emotional toll of aggressive recovery agent harassment and the constant fear of legal action can shatter families and mental peace.

At Loan Free Financial Services, we believe that debt is a financial problem, not a character flaw. We are a dedicated team of legal and financial professionals focused on helping individuals and small business owners resolve their liabilities legally, ethically, and with dignity. From our base at the historic Tis Hazari Court in Delhi, we provide a protective shield and a strategic roadmap for clients across every corner of India.

Our Story: Born from Necessity

Loan Free Financial Services was founded by a group of legal professionals and financial experts who saw a disturbing trend in the Indian banking sector. While the system was efficient at disbursing loans, it lacked an ethical, client-first mechanism to support borrowers during genuine hardship. We witnessed firsthand the intense pressure placed on middle-class families—unauthorized recovery tactics, misleading legal threats, and a complete lack of clear guidance on debt resolution options.

Deciding that enough was enough, our founders combined their deep knowledge of Indian banking norms, RBI guidelines, and litigation support to create a service that didn't just 'settle' debts, but restored lives. We realized that borrowers didn't need shortcuts; they needed a professional voice to negotiate on their behalf and a legal framework to protect their rights.

What began as handling a handful of local cases in Delhi has grown into a pan-India mission. Today, we support clients from Mumbai to Bengaluru, and Chennai to Kolkata, proving that when legal expertise meets human empathy, real change is possible. Our journey is defined by the thousands of peaceful nights we've returned to Indian homes.

Legal & Ethical Practice

Upholding the highest standards of integrity in the Indian debt resolution industry.

Loan Free Financial Services operates with a clear mandate: to provide legal relief within the framework of Indian law. We strictly adhere to the Fair Practices Code for Lenders and the circulars issued by the RBI regarding debt collection.

We do not support or encourage the intentional avoidance of legitimate dues or any activity that seeks to manipulate the banking system. Our commitment is to the honest borrower who needs a legal way to settle and move forward.

What we DO

  • Advise on legal rights as a borrower.
  • Negotiate settlements and restructuring.
  • Protect from recovery harassment.
  • Educate on long-term financial planning.

What we DO NOT do

  • Make false promises of 100% waivers.
  • Encourage default or hiding from lenders.
  • Provide loans or guarantee credit repairs.
  • Bypass any Indian banking regulations.
