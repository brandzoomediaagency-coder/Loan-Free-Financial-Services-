Digital Lending App Harassment Support

Guidance on privacy misuse, threats and contact-list abuse by digital lending apps — including unregulated ones.

  1. Home
  2. Services
  3. Digital Lending App Harassment Support

Quick answer

Some digital lending apps misuse phone permissions to access your contacts and photos, then threaten or shame you. RBI's digital lending guidelines require regulated lenders to follow fair practices and limit data access. You can document the abuse and escalate to the app/lender, the RBI and cybercrime authorities. We help you understand your rights and structure your response.

Predatory loan apps often combine very high charges with aggressive tactics — mass-messaging your contacts, morphed photos, threats and constant calls. These practices can breach data-protection and fair-practice norms, and there are lawful ways to respond.

Who this service is for

  • Borrowers being threatened or shamed by a loan app.
  • People whose contacts, photos or personal data are being misused.
  • Those facing abusive calls or messages from app recovery teams.
  • Anyone unsure whether an app is a regulated lender and what their rights are.

Documents usually required

  • The app name, lender details and your loan/account information.
  • Screenshots of threats, messages and any shaming content.
  • Call logs and records of contact with you or your contacts.
  • Details of the permissions the app requested and any payments made.

Risks & limitations to understand

  • Asserting rights does not erase any genuine, lawfully owed amount.
  • Outcomes of complaints depend on the evidence and authority response.
  • Serious threats or extortion should be reported to the police/cybercrime cell immediately.
  • No specific result can be guaranteed.
Important: We provide guidance, documentation support and coordination. We do not promise harassment will stop immediately. Report serious threats or extortion to the police or the cybercrime portal without delay.

Frequently asked questions

RBI's digital lending guidelines restrict regulated lenders from accessing borrower contacts, media and similar data without a genuine need and consent. Misuse of such access can be reported and escalated.

Preserve evidence (screenshots, call logs), complain to the app/lender's grievance officer and the RBI, and report serious threats or extortion to the police or the national cybercrime portal. We can help you structure these steps.

Unregulated apps may be operating unlawfully. You can report them to the authorities and cybercrime cell. We help you document the issue and understand your options.
