Credit Card Debt Resolution

Credit card debt is one of the most toxic forms of credit in India, with interest rates often exceeding 40% per annum. The "Minimum Amount Due" trap ensures that most your payment goes toward interest, leaving the principal untouched for years. The psychological stress of daily recovery calls and growing statements can be paralyzing.

We specialize in breaking this cycle. Our team negotiates for massive waivers of interest and late payment penalties. We work towards creating lump-sum or staged settlements that allow you to close these high-interest accounts once and for all.

Signs you need help:

  • You only pay the "Minimum Amount Due" every month.
  • You use one credit card's limit to pay another card's bill.
  • Your total card debt is more than 3 months of your income.
  • You receive frequent calls from recovery departments.

Benefits of Structured Settlement:

  • Interest stops accumulating from the day we start representation.
  • Avoid the trap of "settlement" vs "closure" without legal advice.
  • Protection from harassment while negotiations are ongoing.
  • A clear, documented path to being debt-free.

Myth vs Reality

Myth: Settling a credit card will permanently ban you from all banking services.
Reality: While a settlement does impact your CIBIL score temporarily, it is far better than a "Write-off" or "Default." With our guidance, you can follow a post-settlement credit repair strategy to rebuild your score in 12-24 months.

Recovery Agent Harassment Protection

One of the most distressing aspects of debt is dealing with aggressive recovery agents. Many agents resort to unauthorized tactics like calling at odd hours, threatening friends or family, and visiting homes or offices without prior notice. These behaviors are not just unethical; they are often in direct violation of RBI's Fair Practices Code and various Supreme Court judgments.

At Loan Free Financial Services, we act as a shield between you and the harassers. Our goal is to stop all forms of illegal harassment and steer the conversation towards lawful, respectful negotiation. We represent your case to the lenders' nodal officers and ensure that your rights as a borrower are respected at all times.

How we step in:

  • Immediate advisory on how to handle recovery calls.
  • Drafting formal notices to lenders regarding harassment.
  • Direct representation by our legal team to the bank's higher authorities.
  • Guidance on documenting and reporting violations.

What you should stop doing immediately:

  • Do not argue or get provoked into using abusive language back.
  • Do not sign any documents or blank papers provided by agents.
  • Do not share OTPs or sensitive personal data with unknown callers.
  • Do not hide the problem from your family—transparency reduces stress.

Your Rights as a Borrower

  • Agents can only call between 7:00 AM and 7:00 PM.
  • Agents cannot use muscle power or threats of physical harm.
  • Your privacy must be maintained; agents cannot inform your neighbors or relatives about your debt.
  • You have the right to demand the agent's ID card and the bank's authorization letter.

Legal Notice & Court Case Support

Receiving a legal notice or a court summons for debt can be terrifying. Many borrowers make the mistake of ignoring these documents, which can lead to ex-parte orders or severe legal consequences. Whether it's a notice under Section 138 (Cheque Bounce) or a generic demand notice, timely action is critical.

Our legal team at Tis Hazari Court specializes in analyzing these notices and formulating a strategy that protects your interests. We help you understand the legal standing of the claim and explore possibilities of settling the matter even after litigation has begun. Settlement in court (Lok Adalat or through mediation) is often a viable and structured way to close old debts.

When to contact us:

  • After receiving a lawyer's notice or a bank's demand letter.
  • When you receive a summons from a court or a mediation cell.
  • When you are unsure if a legal document you received is genuine.
  • Before a mediation or Lok Adalat hearing.

How we support you:

  • Drafting legally sound and strategic responses to notices.
  • Coordinating with local counsel for representation in court.
  • Negotiating out-of-court settlements during active litigation.
  • Guidance on procedural requirements for debt-related cases.
