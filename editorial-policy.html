Editorial Policy

How Loan Free Financial Services researches, writes, sources and maintains the accuracy of its financial and legal content.

  1. Home
  2. Editorial Policy

Last reviewed: August 2026. This page explains how we produce, source and maintain the content on loanfree.info.

Our purpose

Loan Free Financial Services publishes information about loan settlement, debt resolution, recovery agent rights and related legal processes in India. Our content is intended to help borrowers understand their options and rights — clearly, honestly and without misleading claims.

We are a consultancy, not a lender and not a law firm. The content on this site is for informational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice. Readers should consult qualified professionals for advice specific to their circumstances.

Who writes our content

Content on this site is produced by the advisory and legal coordination team at Loan Free Financial Services, who collectively have experience in:

  • Loan settlement negotiation and case management.
  • Banking and NBFC regulatory compliance under RBI guidelines.
  • Borrower rights under the RBI Fair Practices Code.
  • Indian civil and commercial litigation, including Section 138 NI Act matters.
  • CIBIL and credit bureau dispute processes.

Where applicable, content is reviewed for legal and regulatory accuracy before publication. We do not use AI-generated content without human review and verification.

Our sources

We prioritise official and authoritative sources for regulatory and legal information:

  • Reserve Bank of India (RBI): rbi.org.in — for Fair Practices Code, recovery guidelines, Digital Lending Directions, Ombudsman framework and regulatory circulars.
  • Ministry of Finance / Government of India: For MSME policy, IBC provisions and government-declared relief schemes.
  • Credit Information Bureau (India) Limited: cibil.com — for credit score methodology and dispute processes.
  • National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal: cybercrime.gov.in — for digital lending and online fraud complaint guidance.
  • Indian statutes: Indian Contract Act, 1872; Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881; Indian Penal Code; and other applicable laws.
  • Court judgements: Published judgements of the Supreme Court of India and relevant High Courts where cited.

We do not cite or rely on unverified secondary sources, social media posts or statistics without cross-referencing official data.

What we will not publish

  • Guarantees of specific settlement amounts, CIBIL score improvements or case outcomes — we are transparent that all outcomes depend on individual circumstances and lender policy.
  • Fabricated statistics, client identities or review data.
  • Content that encourages wilful default or misleads borrowers about their legal obligations.
  • Doorway or keyword-stuffed pages designed purely for search manipulation.
  • Content promoting loan-related scams or unregistered entities.

Content review and update process

Financial and legal regulations in India — particularly RBI circulars and court guidance — change periodically. Our commitment:

  • New regulatory developments affecting our core content areas (recovery agent rules, digital lending directions, OTS policies, etc.) are reviewed and incorporated within 30 days of publication.
  • All service and guide pages are reviewed for accuracy at least every 6 months. The dateModified field in our structured data is updated to reflect the most recent substantive review.
  • Blog articles are updated when the underlying regulatory or legal information changes materially. Year references are updated to avoid misleading out-of-date content.
  • Reader corrections: If you identify an error, outdated information or a misleading statement, please email us at info@athenalegalsolutionllp.in with the specific concern. We will investigate and correct within 14 working days if the issue is substantiated.

Disclaimers applicable to all content

Financial and Legal Disclaimer: All content on loanfree.info is for general informational purposes only. It does not constitute legal advice, financial advice or a solicitor-client relationship. Loan Free Financial Services provides consultation, negotiation support, documentation guidance and legal coordination — we are not a lender and do not provide credit. We do not encourage intentional default and do not guarantee any waiver percentage, settlement outcome or CIBIL result. All outcomes vary case by case depending on lender policy, outstanding amount, hardship profile and documentation. For advice specific to your situation, please consult a qualified legal or financial professional.

Advertising and commercial relationships

Loan Free Financial Services does not accept paid placement, sponsored content or third-party advertising on this website. All service recommendations and external links are editorially independent. If this policy changes, we will disclose it prominently on this page.
