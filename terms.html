About our services

Loan Free Financial Services provides consultation, negotiation support, documentation guidance and legal coordination relating to debt resolution and loan settlement. We are not a lender and do not provide loans, credit or refinancing. We do not encourage anyone to intentionally default on their obligations.

No guaranteed outcome

Every case is different. We do not guarantee any specific waiver percentage, settlement amount, timeline or credit-score outcome. Results depend on the lender's policy, your total outstanding amount, your hardship profile and your documentation. Any figures referenced on this site are illustrative and based on internal case records.
