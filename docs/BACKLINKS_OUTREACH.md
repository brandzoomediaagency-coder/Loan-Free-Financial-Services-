## LinkedIn plan (company page + one named spokesperson)

- 2 posts a week for 12 weeks: one explainer (from a site guide, with the link), one practical tip (carousel from an illustration).
- Series: "Know the rule" (one RBI paragraph per post), "Before you pay" (settlement letter checks), "Myths" (e.g. "settlement deletes your debt from CIBIL").
- No client stories without written consent; no outcome claims.
