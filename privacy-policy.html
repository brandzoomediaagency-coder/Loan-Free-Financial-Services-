Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

  1. Home
  2. Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 May 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how Loan Free Financial Services ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects the personal information you share with us when you use this website or our consultation services.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide voluntarily — such as your name, phone number, city/state and email address.
  • Case details you choose to share — such as loan type, outstanding amount, lender and a description of your situation.
  • Technical data — basic usage information your browser sends automatically (for example, device and approximate location), and any analytics your hosting provider records.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide a free consultation.
  • To assess your situation and explain available options.
  • To contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email about your request.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and record-keeping obligations.

WhatsApp & form submissions

When you submit a form on this website, the information is formatted into a message and opened in WhatsApp on your own device so you can send it to us. We receive only what you choose to send. Standard WhatsApp terms and privacy practices also apply.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited details with your lenders or their representatives only when you ask us to assist with negotiation or documentation, and with professional advisors or service providers who help us operate, under appropriate confidentiality.

Data retention & security

We retain your information only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law, and we take reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, by emailing info@athenalegalsolutionllp.in. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Cookies & third parties

This site loads fonts and icons from third-party providers (Google Fonts and a font/icon CDN), which may receive your IP address. We do not use these to identify you personally.

Questions about this policy? Email info@athenalegalsolutionllp.in or call +91 70425 38878.
